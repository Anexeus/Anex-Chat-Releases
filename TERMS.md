# Anex Chat terms and privacy

## Using Anex Chat
Use Anex Chat lawfully. Respect other people’s privacy. Ask before sharing someone else’s personal information. Do not send spam, impersonate others, or use the service to threaten or harass people.

## Your chats and profile
Your device holds your profile and chat history. Selected servers route encrypted messages and transfer files. Operators may receive connection information, including IP addresses and timing, when your device connects. Other participants can save or share content that you send them.

## Calls, backups and updates
Calling can involve relay servers. Direct calls can reveal your IP address to the other caller. You choose where to save backups through Android’s file picker. Keep the backup and its database passphrase safe. The outer ZIP may contain readable attachments. The Android app checks GitHub for releases, so GitHub receives ordinary network request information, including your IP address.

## Other services
Server operators, storage providers and GitHub may have their own terms and privacy policies. Review those when choosing their services. Permissions for camera, microphone and notifications are managed in Android settings.

## Software and support
The app is distributed under AGPL-3.0. Your rights under that licence and applicable law continue to apply. For release information or a source-access request, visit the official Anex Chat releases repository. Use a public issue only for information you are comfortable sharing publicly.
